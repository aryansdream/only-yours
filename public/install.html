Install Only Yours · Android ↗Android 11 or newer · Test build 0.4.5 · chat, calls, camera & sharing
  1. Tap the download button on your phone.
  2. Open the APK and approve Android’s installation prompt. If asked, allow this download source.
  3. Use your phone’s screen lock, create your unique ID and password, then exchange your pairing codes.
  4. Compare your shared security fingerprint with your person.

Photos and files stay on devices and wait locally for a direct connection. Only text and control messages use the temporary encrypted relay queue. Update both devices. No guaranteed 20–50 ms latency. This security implementation is not independently audited. Android notifications require enabling alerts and keeping its connection service running; force-stopping the app stops alerts.
