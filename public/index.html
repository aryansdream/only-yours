Notifications

Optional notifications show “A little love is waiting”, never message content. Android has message alerts, Remember me beep/vibration nudges and incoming-call notifications with Answer/Decline actions. Enable notifications below and allow Android permission. The Firebase push client is included; server authorization is still pending. Until setup is complete, the connection service must remain running. Answering or declining from an alert opens your protected app.

Device storage & lock

History and private keys are encrypted with your password on this browser. Unlock required after switching away or one minute of inactivity. An active Android call continues with an ongoing notification; the chat locks when you return. On a remembered Android device, use your phone’s screen lock. The Android app also requires your phone’s PIN, pattern, password or biometrics. Browser mode uses your app password. Losing browser storage loses your history and device identity.

Local chat storage

Photos and files are sent directly between devices. When direct delivery is unavailable, they wait on your device. Clear chat removes your local copy only.

Direct connections use Google STUN for network discovery. No TURN relay is configured; restrictive networks may prevent photo delivery. Only text and control messages can use the encrypted queue. View once cannot prevent an external camera or a modified client from capturing media. Expiry removes content from this app, including on next unlock after a device was offline. Experimental security implementation. Not independently audited. No forward secrecy. Account/pairing metadata remains on the relay; queued ciphertext expires after 7 days. No cloud message history or key recovery.
